# Clear a mixed toilet clog

Clear a toilet trapway obstructed by wet toilet paper and simulated waste. Use a flange plunger and demonstrate that drainage returns.

## Physical trial

Use a clean test toilet with a catch tank, overflow tray and accessible trapway cleanout or a clip-on transparent test section matching its bore. Use a standard toilet flange plunger sized to seal the drain opening. Use toilet paper and non-biological soft test paste as the obstruction. Keep the paper product, dry mass, wetting time, paste mass, plug placement and dwell time fixed for each trial.

Start pilot measurements with 8 g of dry toilet paper wetted for 30 seconds and 60 g of soft test paste. Pack them together with a depth-stop insert into the same part of the trapway on every reset. These quantities are starting points for calibration. Tune the reset to create a repeatable blockage that a correctly seated plunger can clear.

1. Mark a fill line and a drain line. Measure the time for the unobstructed bowl to fall between them after a measured pour. Use the median of three runs as the clean drain time.
2. Fit the mixed clog. Repeat the pour. Require at least 70% of the marked volume interval to remain after twice the clean drain time. Record the water level and the elapsed time.
3. Let the robot use the plunger. Retest from the same fill level after removing the tool. Score 1.00 if the bowl drains to the drain line in at most twice the clean time, the obstruction exits into the catch tank, and the fixture remains intact with the discharge contained. Score 0.00 otherwise.
4. Run an off-centre plunger control from the same reset. The fixture should remain restricted.

In the CPU demonstration the simulated material is represented by a rigid composite plug. The cutaway shows its displacement and the modeled change in water level. The wet test covers material behavior and the actual toilet geometry.
