This is a scale footwell on a workbench. All dimensions below are in mm. Measure x from the heel edge of the mat toward the pedals, and y from the mat centre toward the tunnel. The pull lip faces the door opening at the heel. Photograph a ruler beside the completed fixture.

## Debris and force matching

Prepare 48 reusable non-friable oval pellets from a single batch of cured modelling clay or filled resin. Target mass 0.8 g each, within 0.15 g. Make 36 brown loose pieces with their long axis 7.2–12.8 mm, width 81% and height 70% of the long axis. Make 12 terracotta bonded pieces 9.6 × 11.6 × 8.8 mm, each with a flat bonding face. Permanently mark A01–A12 on their upper faces. Keep the loose population unmarked. Store the two sets in separate numbered wells. Reject fragments and replace damaged pieces before reset.

Use the heel-edge coordinates in reset.csv (mat centreline y=0). On a clean, dry mat, apply one small disc of removable low-tack sheet adhesive beneath each numbered piece through a reusable stencil. Start with 3 mm discs punched from the same roll. Apply 2 N downward to each for 2 seconds, then allow 60 seconds of dwell. Make six witness bonds on an offcut of the same EPDM from the same cleaned batch. Use a 0–5 N force gauge with 0.01 N resolution in a horizontal stand. Push each pellet 2 mm above its base at approximately 5 mm/s along the brush direction. Record peak force up to first slip. The nominal simulation breaks at **1.00 N of net in-plane bristle load**. The proposed physical batch tolerance is 0.90–1.10 N per coupon. If any coupon falls outside it, adjust the disc diameter on a fresh set, repeat the check, and log the roll, diameter, dwell, ambient temperature and all six readings. No bench force measurements are included yet. Place the 36 loose pieces without adhesive using the CSV and lift the placement stencil vertically. Photograph the numbered clumps and reference marks before each attempt.

## Transfer, count and reset

Put a removable liner under the rig and a fine mesh screen over exposed vents. Protect rails and wrist seams with fitted flexible boots, leaving finger pads, valves and vents functional. Keep all grit inside the lined area and remove it with the robot stopped. Start with an empty, tagged basket and tray. Verify two seated sockets, 12 intact marked bonds and 36 loose pieces against the setup photograph.

After release of the mat, record both sockets' disengagement on video. At the end switch off suction, remove the catch basket into a white counting pan, and empty the tray into a second pan. Empty residual material from the hose into a third **uncollected** pan. Two observers independently tally marked IDs and loose counts in each pan, then inspect the mat, footwell and liner. A marked pellet still attached to its original site is unbrushed. A displaced marked pellet counts as loosened even if it never reached a pan. If it lies on its starting mark, check the continuous brush video and gently tilt the isolated mat over the third pan only *after* final tray and vacuum counts are locked. If prior detachment cannot be determined, record the trial as unreadable. Account for all 48 across disjoint locations. Missing IDs or broken pellets invalidate the count. Confirm both clips reseated after release, mat flat, no hand support, and both pedals clear. Archive the independent counts, images and coupon measurements.
